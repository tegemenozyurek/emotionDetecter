    How it works›
    1. DetectMediaPipe finds faces, eyes, nose and mouth
    2. Aligneach face is rotated and scaled onto one template
    3. Classifya CNN trained from scratch scores 7 emotions
    4. Smoothfaces are tracked and their scores averaged over time
    Models›
    Models trained from scratch on FER+ and RAF-DB · source & results

    A non-commercial learning project. Images and video are processed only on your device and are never uploaded or stored. Predictions are a model's guess about a facial expression, not a judgement of how someone feels.
